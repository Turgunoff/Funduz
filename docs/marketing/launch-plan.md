# Launch Plan

## Maqsad

2026 iyul–avgust oylarida Funduz public launch qilish va birinchi 30 kunda **5,000+ ro'yxatdan o'tish** va **10+ aktiv loyiha** ga erishish.

---

## Bosqichlar

### Pre-launch (T-90 dan T-1 gacha)

#### T-90 ... T-60 — Seed loyihalar to'plash
**Maqsad:** 5–10 ta sifatli loyiha topish va "launch partner" sifatida bog'lash.

**Qayerdan topish:**
- IT Park rezidentlari (1,500+ kompaniya)
- TUMARIS, Astrum Innovations va boshqa startup acceleratorlar
- Indie maker'lar (Telegram kanallari: @uzdev, @uzitcommunity)
- Dizaynerlar va illustratorlar
- Tech YouTuber'lar (texnologik mahsulotlar uchun)
- Kreatorlar (musiqachi, yozuvchi, art)

**Taklif:**
- Komissiya 0% (birinchi 6 oy)
- Free PR va featured listing
- Founder bilan shaxsiy kampaniya coaching
- Logo va branding yordami (kerakli bo'lsa)

#### T-60 ... T-30 — Kontent va PR tayyorlash
- Brand guidelines tugatish
- Landing page'ni optimizatsiya qilish
- Press kit (logo, founder bio, screenshot, deck)
- Demo video (60 soniya)
- Social media akkauntlar (Telegram, Instagram, X, LinkedIn)
- Birinchi 20 ta blog post (SEO uchun)

#### T-30 ... T-7 — Pre-launch list
- Email pre-launch list (waitlist)
- "Coming soon" sahifa
- Telegram kanal launch (kontent strategiya)
- Influencer'lar bilan bog'lanish (T-7 ga deal'lar tugallangan bo'lsin)

#### T-7 ... T-1 — Final
- Bug bash va load testing
- Yuridik tekshirish (terms, privacy)
- To'lov sandbox → production o'tish
- Press relisni jurnalistlarga ertaroq yuborish (embargoed)
- Founder e'lon postini tayyorlash

**Vaqt:** Seshanba yoki Chorshanba ertalab (08:00 UZB)
- Telegram kanal e'loni
- LinkedIn post (Eldor sahifasidan)
- Instagram + Stories
- Telegram chat'larda e'lon (prepared list)
- Press relis publikatsiyasi:
  - Daryo.uz
  - Pivot.uz
  - Spot.uz
  - Gazeta.uz
  - Kun.uz
- IT Park rasmiy kanali (agar imkon bo'lsa)
- Influencer'lar 3 soat ichida o'z postlarini ulashadi

### Post-launch (T+1 ... T+30)

#### Hafta 1
- Real-time monitoring (server, signups, errors)
- Foydalanuvchi feedback'iga 1 soat ichida javob
- Birinchi pledge hodisani Telegram'da nishonlash
- Bug fixlar — eng tezkor bosqich

#### Hafta 2–4
- Birinchi muvaffaqiyatli loyiha — case study
- "Seed loyihalar" ning haftalik update'lari
- Influencer marketing 2-to'lqin
- Targeted ads (Facebook, Instagram)
- Telegram bot statistikalari
