# Success Stories — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a Success Stories page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. Showcase projects that successfully reached their funding goals and what happened after.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Inspirational, celebratory, human
- Rounded corners, bold typography, photography-rich

## Page purpose
Inspire new creators and backers by showcasing real Funduz projects that succeeded. Build trust through social proof.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header.

**Hero section:**
- Breadcrumb: Home › Success Stories
- Huge heading: "Ideas that became reality" (64px, `font-black`)
- Subheading: "Meet the Uzbek creators who turned their dreams into funded projects on Funduz." (20px, gray-500)
- Three rotating hero stats (big numbers): "250+ funded projects • 15B UZS raised • 12,500+ happy backers"

**Featured success story (hero card):**
- Full-width card with two-column split
- Left (60%): large photo of the creator with their finished product
- Right (40%): "Featured story" green pill, project title (`font-black`, 32px), funding result ("Raised 180% of goal in 14 days" — large green text), short excerpt, creator name + role, "Read the full story" button (filled dark green)

**Filter chips:**
- "All / Technology / Art / Education / Ecology / Social / Business / Health"
- Active chip filled green

**Success story grid:**
- 3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile
- Each card:
  - Cover photo (rounded-2xl top)
  - Category pill overlay
  - "Funded" green check badge top-right
  - Project title (`font-black`, 20px)
  - Result highlight: "**156%** of goal" (big green)
  - Short 1-line quote from creator: "Funduz changed my life."
  - Stats row: raised amount, backers count, funded date
  - Creator mini-row: small avatar + name
  - "Read story" arrow link
- 9–12 cards, "Load more" button at the bottom

**Impact section (full-width dark green):**
- Heading in white: "Our impact so far"
- 4 big stat cards (white text on dark green):
  - Projects funded
  - Money raised (UZS)
  - Backers involved
  - Cities reached
- Below: small world map of Uzbekistan highlighting cities with dots sized by project count

**Testimonial carousel:**
- Heading: "What creators say"
- Large rounded-3xl cards in a carousel (swipe on mobile)
- Each: large photo, long quote in green italic, creator name, project name, funding result

**CTA section (bottom):**
- Light green card, full-width
- Heading: "Your idea could be next"
- Subtext: "Thousands of backers are waiting to support the next great project from Uzbekistan."
- Buttons: "Start your project" (filled dark green) and "Read the creator guide" (outlined)

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: wide editorial grid, featured card prominent
- Tablet: 2-column grid, featured card stacked
- Mobile: single column, carousel swipes horizontally

## Visual notes
- Lots of real authentic photography — no stock
- Celebratory but not over-the-top
- Use green consistently for "success" indicators (check badges, percentages, quotes)

## Copy language
English, inspirational, story-driven. Let the creators' words carry the page.
