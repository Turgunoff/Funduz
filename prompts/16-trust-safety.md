# Trust & Safety — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a Trust & Safety page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. Explain how Funduz protects backers and creators through escrow, verification, refund policies, and fraud prevention.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Serious, reassuring, confident — but still warm
- Rounded corners, bold typography, strong visual metaphors (shields, locks, checkmarks)

## Page purpose
Build trust with visitors by clearly explaining how Funduz keeps their money, data, and projects safe. This is the page a cautious backer reads before their first pledge.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header.

**Hero section:**
- Breadcrumb: Home › Trust & Safety
- Two-column (60/40 desktop):

Left:
- Small green pill: "Trust & Safety"
- Huge heading: "Your money is safe with Funduz" (56–64px, `font-black`)
- Subheading: "We use escrow, verification, and the all-or-nothing funding model to protect every single pledge." (20px, gray-600)
- Three small trust badges in a row: "🛡️ Escrow protected • ✅ Verified creators • 💸 Refund guarantee"

Right:
- Large illustration: a shield with Funduz green gradient, or a lock with floating checkmarks, or a safe with coins
- Rounded-3xl container, light green gradient background

**Key principles strip:**
- 4 large cards in a row (2x2 on tablet, 1 col on mobile)
- Each card: icon in green circle, heading, 2-line description
  1. 🛡️ **Escrow protection** — "Your money is held securely until the project reaches its goal."
  2. ✅ **Verified creators** — "Every creator is verified before launching."
  3. 💸 **Refund guarantee** — "If a project doesn't succeed, you get 100% of your money back."
  4. 🔒 **Secure payments** — "All transactions protected with bank-grade encryption."

**How escrow works section (step by step):**
- Heading: "How escrow protects your pledge"
- Horizontal 4-step process diagram:
  1. **You pledge** — Money is authorized on your card
  2. **Held in escrow** — Funds are locked in a secure account, not given to the creator yet
  3. **Goal reached?** — If yes, funds released. If no, fully refunded.
  4. **Protected delivery** — Creator delivers, you receive
- Each step: green circle with number, title, description, connecting line with arrow
- Below the diagram: a light green callout box with an info icon: "Funduz uses [Licensed Payment Provider] for escrow services, regulated by the Central Bank of Uzbekistan."

**All-or-nothing section:**
- Two-column:
- Left: heading "All-or-nothing funding", explanation paragraph, bullet list of benefits
- Right: illustration of two scales — one with "Goal reached → funds released" and one with "Goal not reached → refund"

**Creator verification section:**
- Heading: "Every creator is verified"
- Bullet list of what Funduz checks:
  - Government-issued ID
  - Bank account ownership
  - Phone number verification
  - Background check for high-goal projects
- Small green badge: "Look for the ✅ Verified badge on every project"

**Refund policy section:**
- Heading: "Our refund guarantee"
- Three scenarios as cards:
  1. "Project didn't reach its goal" — "Full refund within 3 business days"
  2. "Creator cancels the project" — "Full refund within 7 business days"
  3. "Creator fails to deliver" — "Refund via our dispute resolution process"

**Reporting section:**
- Heading: "See something suspicious?"
- Subtext: "We take fraud seriously. Report any concerning project or user and our team will investigate within 24 hours."
- Two buttons: "Report a project" (outlined dark green) and "Contact trust team" (filled dark green)

**Security details section (tech-focused, for trust):**
- Heading: "Technical security measures"
- Grid of 6 small cards, each with icon + label:
  - 🔒 256-bit SSL encryption
  - 🛡️ PCI-DSS compliant
  - 🔑 Two-factor authentication
  - 🚫 Fraud detection AI
  - 📊 Regular security audits
  - 🇺🇿 Data hosted in Uzbekistan

**FAQ section:**
- Heading: "Trust & Safety FAQ"
- Accordion with 6–8 questions:
  - "What if the creator runs away with the money?"
  - "How long does a refund take?"
  - "Is my card information safe?"
  - "What happens if I have a dispute?"
  - etc.

**CTA section (bottom):**
- Light green card
- Heading: "Have a question about safety?"
- Subtext: "Our trust team is here to help."
- Button: "Contact trust team"

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: wide editorial layout, 4-card grids, horizontal step diagram
- Tablet: 2-column grids, stacked diagrams
- Mobile: single column, vertical step diagram, compact cards

## Visual notes
- Use strong trust iconography throughout (shields, locks, checkmarks, verification badges)
- Green is the trust color — lean into it
- Authentic, confident tone — never defensive

## Copy language
English, clear and authoritative. Short, reassuring sentences. Lead with the user's concern, then resolve it.
