# FAQ / Help Center — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a FAQ / Help Center page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. Creators launch projects and backers pledge money in UZS. All-or-nothing funding with escrow protection.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Modern, trustworthy, optimistic feel
- Rounded corners (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`), bold black headings (`font-black`)
- Generous whitespace, soft shadows, white cards on near-white body

## Page purpose
Let first-time visitors quickly find answers about how Funduz works, how to back a project, and what happens with their money — without contacting support.

## Layout

**Header:** Sticky top nav — FUNDUZ logo (left, green), links "Explore / How It Works / Community" (center), language switcher + Login button (right). Mobile: hamburger.

**Hero section (top of page):**
- Large heading: "How can we help you?" (48–56px, `font-black`, dark gray)
- Subheading: "Find answers to the most common questions about Funduz" (18px, gray-500)
- Big rounded search input with magnifying glass icon and placeholder "Search for a question…"
- Light green gradient background or decorative shapes

**Category tabs / chips row:**
Pill-shaped buttons horizontally scrollable on mobile:
- "All" (active, filled green)
- "General"
- "For Creators"
- "For Backers"
- "Payments & Refunds"
- "Security & Trust"

**FAQ accordion section:**
- Vertical list of accordion items inside white rounded cards
- Each item: question on the left (bold, 17px), chevron icon on the right
- When opened: smooth reveal with answer text (gray-700), optional bullet list, optional link to related page
- Alternating white and very-light-green backgrounds OR all white with subtle border
- Approximately 8–10 visible items per category

**Sample questions to include:**
1. What is Funduz and how does it work?
2. How do I back a project?
3. When am I charged for my pledge?
4. What happens if a project doesn't reach its goal?
5. How does Funduz protect my money?
6. How do I start my own project?
7. What fees does Funduz charge?
8. How are project creators verified?
9. Can I get a refund?
10. Which payment methods are supported? (Payme, Click, bank card)

**Bottom CTA section:**
- Rounded card with light green background
- Heading: "Still have questions?"
- Subtext: "Our team usually replies within a few hours."
- Two buttons side by side: "Contact Us" (filled dark green) and "Join Telegram" (outlined)

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer with 4 columns (Product / Categories / Company / Legal), copyright, social links.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: max-width 960px centered accordion
- Tablet: 100% width with padding
- Mobile: category chips horizontally scrollable, accordions full-width, search input sticky under header

## Copy language
All UI copy in English. Warm, helpful tone. Avoid jargon.
