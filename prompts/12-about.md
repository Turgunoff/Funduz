# About Us — UI Prompt

## Context
Design an About Us page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. Tell the Funduz story, introduce the team, share the mission, and build trust with visitors.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Warm, human, story-driven, visually rich
- Rounded corners, bold typography, editorial layout, lots of photography

## Page purpose
Explain who Funduz is, why it exists, the team behind it, and what the platform has achieved. Build trust and emotional connection.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header.

**Hero section (top):**
- Breadcrumb: Home › About
- Full-width with light green background or gradient
- Huge headline (2 lines): "We're building the future of **crowdfunding in Uzbekistan**" (bold black, primary word in green)
- Subheadline: "Funduz helps Uzbek creators bring ideas to life by connecting them with a community of backers who believe in their vision." (20px, gray-600, max-width 680px)
- Supporting photo: a warm, authentic image of Uzbek creators/team/projects (rounded-3xl, large)

**Mission statement section:**
- Two-column: large heading on the left ("Our mission"), a paragraph on the right explaining the mission in warm, first-person voice
- Pull quote in green: "Every great project starts with someone who believes in it."

**Key stats strip (full-width, dark green background, white text):**
- 4 large stat cards:
  - 250+ funded projects
  - 12,500+ backers
  - 15B UZS raised
  - 14 cities reached
- Each stat has a large number (`font-black`, 56px), label below, and a small icon

**Story / Timeline section:**
- Heading: "Our story"
- Vertical timeline with 4–6 milestones:
  - 2024 — "An idea is born in Tashkent"
  - 2025 — "First 10 projects funded"
  - 2026 — "Expansion across Uzbekistan"
- Each milestone: green dot, year, title, short description, optional photo
- Zigzag layout alternating left/right on desktop

**Values section:**
- Heading: "What we believe in"
- 3–4 value cards with icons:
  1. 🌱 Trust — "Every transaction is protected by escrow"
  2. 🤝 Community — "We succeed when creators succeed"
  3. 💡 Innovation — "Bringing world-class tools to Uzbek creators"
  4. 🇺🇿 Local first — "Built for Uzbekistan, by Uzbekistan"
- White cards, rounded-2xl, soft shadow, icon in green circle at top

**Team section:**
- Heading: "Meet the team"
- Grid of team member cards (3–4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile)
- Each card:
  - Large circular photo
  - Name (`font-black`)
  - Role (green)
  - One-line bio
  - Small row of social icons (Telegram, LinkedIn)

**Press / Partners strip:**
- Heading: "Trusted by"
- Grayscale row of 5–8 logos (press mentions or partners)

**CTA section (bottom):**
- Light green card, full-width
- Heading: "Ready to bring your idea to life?"
- Subtext: "Join the growing community of creators and backers on Funduz."
- Two buttons: "Start a project" (filled dark green) and "Explore projects" (outlined)

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: generous editorial layout, alternating zigzag for story timeline
- Tablet: tighter spacing, 2-column grids
- Mobile: single column, stacked sections, larger touch targets

## Visual notes
- Use lots of authentic Uzbek imagery
- Photography should feel warm and human, not stocky
- Large typography, confident statements

## Copy language
English, first-person plural ("we"), warm and confident, story-driven.
