# For Creators — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a "For Creators" landing page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. This is a marketing/guide page that convinces potential creators to launch their project on Funduz and shows them how.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Motivational, empowering, clear
- Rounded corners, bold typography, visually rich, conversion-focused

## Page purpose
Convince creators (artists, entrepreneurs, students, innovators) to launch their project on Funduz by showing the benefits, the process, and the tools. This page should feel like a premium "sales" landing page.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header with a prominent "Start a project" CTA button on the right (instead of just "Login").

**Hero section (full-width):**
- Background: light green gradient or subtle pattern
- Two-column (55% / 45% desktop):

Left:
- Small green pill label: "For Creators"
- Huge headline: "Turn your idea into a **funded project**" (primary word in green, 64–72px, `font-black`)
- Subheadline: "Join 250+ Uzbek creators who have raised over 15 billion UZS on Funduz. No upfront costs. Get paid when you hit your goal." (20px, gray-600, max-width 520px)
- Two primary CTAs:
  1. "Start your project" — filled dark green, large, rounded-xl, `font-bold`
  2. "Watch how it works (2 min)" — outlined with play icon
- Trust strip below buttons: "⭐ 4.8/5 creator rating • 🛡️ Escrow protected • 💸 Only 5% fee on success"

Right:
- Large hero image: a creator working on their project (authentic Uzbek photo) inside a rounded-3xl card
- Decorative floating cards showing social proof — a "Funded in 12 days!" chip, a mini project card, a "+1,240 backers" notification

**Benefits section:**
- Heading: "Why creators choose Funduz"
- 3-column grid of benefit cards:
  1. 🎯 "Reach the right audience" — thousands of active Uzbek backers
  2. 💰 "No money at risk" — you only pay fees when you succeed
  3. 🛡️ "Escrow protection" — backers trust us, and they'll trust you
  4. 📊 "Built-in analytics" — track your progress in real time
  5. 🤝 "Dedicated support" — our team helps you every step of the way
  6. 🌍 "Local-first" — translations, UZS payments, Uzbek support
- White cards with icons in green circles, soft shadow, rounded-2xl

**How it works section (step-by-step):**
- Heading: "Launch in 4 simple steps"
- Horizontal timeline with 4 steps (vertical on mobile):
  1. **Plan** — Define your idea, goal, and rewards
  2. **Build** — Create your project page with our guided editor
  3. **Launch** — Share with your community and reach new backers
  4. **Deliver** — Get funded and bring your idea to life
- Each step: large number in green circle, title, short description, illustration or screenshot

**Features showcase (zigzag alternating rows):**
- 3–4 feature rows, alternating image left/right:
  1. "Powerful project editor" — rich text, images, video, reward tiers. Screenshot on the right.
  2. "Real-time analytics dashboard" — track views, pledges, conversion. Screenshot on the left.
  3. "Flexible reward tiers" — set up limited and tiered rewards easily.
  4. "Built for Uzbekistan" — local payments (Payme, Click), UZS currency, Uzbek/Russian/English UI.
- Each row has a heading, 2–3 bullet benefits, and a large rounded-2xl screenshot/mockup

**Pricing / fees section:**
- Heading: "Simple, fair pricing"
- Large card showing: "5% platform fee + payment processing" with an asterisk "Only charged if your project succeeds"
- Comparison row: "No monthly fees. No upfront costs. No hidden charges."

**Success stories strip:**
- Heading: "Creators who made it"
- 3 testimonial cards: creator photo, quote, project name, funding result
- Link: "See all success stories"

**FAQ accordion (short):**
- Heading: "Common questions from creators"
- 5–6 key questions with answers
- Link: "See full FAQ"

**Final CTA section:**
- Full-width dark green background
- Heading (white): "Ready to bring your idea to life?"
- Subtext (white/80): "It only takes 10 minutes to set up your project page."
- Two buttons: "Start your project" (white bg, green text) and "Talk to our team" (outlined white)

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: wide generous layout, zigzag features, 3-column grids
- Tablet: 2-column grids, stacked features
- Mobile: single column, compact hero, vertical timeline, bottom CTA sticky bar

## Visual notes
- This is a **high-conversion** landing page — visual hierarchy matters
- Strong CTAs at top, middle, and bottom
- Authentic creator photography
- Product screenshots/mockups should look polished

## Copy language
English, empowering and practical. Use "you / your" voice. Short, punchy headlines.
