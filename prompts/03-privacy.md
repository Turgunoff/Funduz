# Privacy Policy — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a Privacy Policy page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. Like the Terms of Service page, this is a legal document with a focus on readability.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`, light green: `#f0fdf4`
- Clean, trustworthy, matches Funduz identity
- Rounded corners, bold black headings, generous whitespace

## Page purpose
Explain what data Funduz collects, how it is used, stored, and protected — in a clear, scannable format with a sticky table of contents.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header.

**Page header:**
- Breadcrumb: Home › Legal › Privacy Policy
- Large title: "Privacy Policy"
- Subtitle: "Last updated: April 14, 2026"
- Short intro paragraph: "At Funduz, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights."

**Key points hero strip (above main content):**
4 small cards in a row, each with an icon and a short line:
1. 🛡️ "We never sell your data"
2. 🔒 "Payment info handled by certified processors"
3. 📧 "You can delete your account anytime"
4. 🇺🇿 "Data stored in compliance with Uzbek law"
Cards: white, rounded-2xl, subtle shadow, icon in green circle.

**Main two-column layout (desktop):**

Left (sticky 25% TOC):
- Heading "Contents"
- Anchor links to sections, active one highlighted green

Right (main 75% content):
- Sections:
  1. Information we collect
  2. How we use your information
  3. Sharing with third parties
  4. Cookies and tracking
  5. Data retention
  6. Your rights (access, correct, delete, export)
  7. Security measures
  8. Children's privacy
  9. International data transfers
  10. Changes to this policy
  11. Contact us
- Each section: bold dark-green heading, paragraphs, occasional tables or bullet lists
- Callout boxes in light green for user rights ("You have the right to…")
- Comfortable reading width (~680px)

**Contact card at bottom:**
"Questions about your privacy? Email us at privacy@funduz.uz" with a button.

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: two-column with sticky TOC, 4-card hero strip
- Tablet: hero cards in 2x2, TOC as dropdown
- Mobile: hero cards stacked, TOC as accordion

## Copy language
English, clear and plain. No legalese where avoidable. Empathetic tone.
