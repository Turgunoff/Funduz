# Terms of Service — UI Prompt

## Context
Design a Terms of Service page for **Funduz**, a crowdfunding platform for Uzbekistan. This is a legal document page — readability and clear navigation are the priorities.

## Brand
- Primary green: `#1a946b`, dark green: `#0f5238`
- Clean, document-like feel but still matching the warm Funduz visual identity
- Rounded corners, generous whitespace, readable serif or clean sans-serif for body

## Page purpose
Present the full Terms of Service document in a scannable, readable way with a sticky table of contents for quick navigation.

## Layout

**Header:** Standard Funduz sticky header (logo, nav, language switcher, Login).

**Page header:**
- Breadcrumb: Home › Legal › Terms of Service
- Large title: "Terms of Service" (48px, `font-black`)
- Subtitle: "Last updated: April 14, 2026" (gray-500)
- Thin horizontal divider

**Main two-column layout (desktop):**

Left column (sticky, 25% width):
- "Table of Contents" heading
- Vertical list of anchor links to each section
- Active section highlighted in green with a small left border accent
- Below the TOC: a small "Download PDF" ghost button

Right column (main content, 75% width):
- Long-form document with clear section headings
- Sections to include:
  1. Introduction and acceptance
  2. Definitions
  3. Eligibility
  4. User accounts
  5. Creating a project
  6. Backing a project (pledges)
  7. Fees and payments
  8. All-or-nothing funding model
  9. Refunds
  10. Prohibited content and behavior
  11. Intellectual property
  12. Disputes
  13. Limitation of liability
  14. Changes to these terms
  15. Contact
- Each section: `font-black` green heading, paragraph text (gray-700), occasional bullet lists, subtle horizontal dividers between sections
- Callout boxes (light green background, rounded, with an info icon) for important clauses
- Comfortable line-height (1.7), max text width ~680px for readability

**Bottom section:**
- Small card: "Have questions about these terms? Contact us" with a button linking to Contact page

**Footer:** Standard Funduz footer.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: two-column with sticky TOC
- Tablet: TOC collapses into a dropdown at the top
- Mobile: TOC becomes a collapsible accordion above the content

## Copy language
English, formal but plain-language legal tone. No jargon where avoidable.
